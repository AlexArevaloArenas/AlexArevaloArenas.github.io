## What is Growing Fear?
Growing Fear is a spooky resource management and farming game made for the UJI Game Jam – Spooky Edition 2023. In this game, you play as a poor Cthulhian office worker who has been tasked with frightening cities using monstrous plants. [Play now!](https://hollowblink.itch.io/growing-fear)
